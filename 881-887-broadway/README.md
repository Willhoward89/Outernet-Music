# 881–887 Broadway / 113–115 Fifth Avenue / 9–13 East 18th Street

*The Palimpsest of a Metropolitan Corner: A Scholarly Chronicle* — Griffith Thomas's Arnold
Constable store of 1868–69, its 1877 cast-iron extension to Fifth Avenue, the Presbyterian church
that stood on the corner before it, and the century of tenancies that followed.

Seventeen chapters, from the ground lease of 1820 to the restaurant called America.

**All 82 quotes are sourced; 80 of them are clickable** and open their scanned page.

**Repository path:** `brick-cakes/881-887-broadway/`

## Firsts for the series

  carries six views of the corner, each opening full size.

  document.

## How it reads

**The citation chart is on the left; the photographs are on the right.**

- **Left — the citation chart.** It follows you as you scroll, and jumps to the matching entry when
  you hover a highlight. Click a number to go to that quote in the text, or *scan* to open the page.
  It divides into the document's seventeen chapters.
- **Centre — the text.** 82 highlighted quotes.
- **Right — the views.** Six photographs under the heading *Views of the corner*; click any to
  enlarge.

The side columns are sticky and stack above the text below ~1360px.

Notes for the record:

- **Two pages are still without a scan** — White & Willensky, *AIA Edition 1*, 1968, p. 94 and
  Murphy, *Murph Guide Closed Bars*, 2023, p. 15, one quote each. Both are sourced and
  colour-coded; drop the pages into `assets/scans/` under those exact names and rebuild to reach
  82 of 82.
- **The Stokes page number was recovered from the scan.** The supplied chart left that cell blank;
  the page's running head reads `930 THE ICONOGRAPHY OF MANHATTAN ISLAND`, and the Cedar Street
  Presbyterian entry on it carries the quoted text, so the citation reads p. 930.
- **Culyer p. 1 is a reference card, not a page image** — it shows the quote and its attribution on
  a styled card. The highlight opens it, but it is evidence of a different kind from the other 32.
- **Seven cartographic pages came from the series scan pool** — Perris 1853 p. 56, Dripps 1867 p. 7,
  Robinson 1885 p. 15, Bromley 1897 p. 19, the 1916 Atlas p. 54, the 1930 Land Book p. 53 and the
  1955 Land Book p. 43, so chapter XIII was clickable before any new scan arrived. The series spells
  these citations both with and without the year; this document uses the no-year form throughout,
  because that is how the existing scan files are named.
- **The sources disagree on the construction date.** Gayle & Gillon p. 164 has Griffith Thomas
  designing it in 1868 and Hennessey p. 43 has the firm hiring him in 1868, while White & Willensky
  p. 94 dates the Broadway front to 1873. Each is quoted as printed; the disagreement is the
  sources', not the transcription's.
- **The text preserves its sources' own errors**, as printed: *The Filth Avenue Presbyterian
  Church*, *By 1000 this uptown migration*, *Portbait … Mezzotinio*, *presented by die buyer*,
  *A. B. Barries & Co.*, *southwest comer*, *Of them all. the building*, *a proposition w a s made*.
- **Two of the six photographs are book plates**, numbered 48 and 49, rather than street views.
- **The assets are encoded smaller than the rest of the series.** At full quality this folder came to
  42 MB, too large to upload through the browser, so the audio is 24 kbps mono (not 40 kbps) and the
  scans are capped at 1500px at quality 78 (not 1800px at 86). Checked against the densest small
  type in the set &mdash; the 1865 Trow directory page &mdash; the fine print is unaffected. The
  78-page PDF keeps every scan as an appendix and is 11 MB of the 28.

To replace or add a scan, drop it into `assets/scans/` named exactly as the citation reads and
rebuild.
