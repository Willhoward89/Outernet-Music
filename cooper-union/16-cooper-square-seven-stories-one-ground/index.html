16 Cooper SquareSeven Stories, One Ground

Every line traces one lot through over 170 years — nine to eleven crowded brick dwellings, stores, and stables on the maps from 1853 to 1897, cleared for a single seven‑story building in 1901 that housed Browning King & Co. and then the drug distributor Schieffelin & Company, and today holds Lost City Arts, its shelves full of Jacobsen, Wegner, Bertoia, and Calder.
