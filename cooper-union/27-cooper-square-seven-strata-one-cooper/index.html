27 Cooper SquareSeven Strata, One Cooper

Every line traces one lot through over 150 years — a single dwelling on the 1853 Perris maps, swelling to twelve structures by 1897 and seven by 1955, the cold‑water flophouse LeRoi Jones warmed in 1962 to write Blues People, later Village Karaoke’s private rooms, before all seven strata were pressed flat for the building raised in 2006.
