Inspired by proto rap / jazz‑hop

Every line traces one corner through over a century — the 1853 Perris maps, St. Mark’s Hotel rising through the decades, and the legendary Five Spot, where Ornette Coleman’s ten‑week run broke jazz open in 1959, on through the bars and bagel shops that followed once the club came down in 1962. 14

“where Monk and Mingus, Coleman, Taylor tore the chord apart, free jazz shaking loose the room, the ten-week burning start — Ornette in fifty-nine, November, alto in his hand”

The Five Spot

"alto saxophonist Ornette Coleman took the jazz world by storm in November 1959, when he appeared at the Five Spot Club in Manhattan. The 10 week performance at the Five Spot introduced Coleman's use of improvised interplay... His approach, which became known as 'free jazz,' shook the jazz world." [Preston & Esposito. "(2005) Manhattans Musical Heritage," p. 87.]
