1 Cooper SquareMorgens of the Valley

Every line traces one valley through more than 370 years — a 1654 Dutch land grant for “25 morgens of land… with the valley,” Kirk’s Dining Saloon by 1850, the legendary Five Spot just across the street where Ornette Coleman broke jazz open in 1959, and the building raised here in 1985. Kirk's Dining Saloon

In the center of each table of an eating house must always be the revolving castor, its thin silver or nickel plating rapidly disappearing, its bottles filled with mustard, vinegar, ketchup, salt, and red and black pepper respectively."
