2 Cooper SquareStore Under

Every line traces one corner through more than 380 years — a 1643–1647 land grant to freed Black New Yorkers, one of the first free quarters on Manhattan, Otto Grim’s 1664 patent confirmed by Stuyvesant, nine crowded brick buildings by the 1850s including H. W. Hoops Confectioner, and the glass‑and‑brick rental tower raised here in 2009. Sourced line by line below.
