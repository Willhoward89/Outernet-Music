Every line traces one barroom address through two centuries of last calls — an empty 1815 lot, Old Man Flood’s saloon, the Astor Place Hotel, Anarchy Café’s belly dancers, and the Continental, where Joey Ramone played his final show before the wrecking ball. Sourced line by line below.

23 Third Avenue

"In the 1930 Land Book of the Borough of Manhattan, on 23 Third Avenue, there is reference to 1 Structure; a 5 story Building with Store." Bromley. 1930 Land Book of the Borough of Manhattan, p. 40.

“where Joey Ramone played his last, December two-thousand, and then — / two-eighteen, the music dead, the bar hung on through August light, / the building due to be razed, bearing walls relieved of their right”

The Continental

"Though the Continental no longer hosted live music, the bar was still open through August 2018, after which the building was due to be razed... Joey Ramone played his last gig here on December 11, 2000." Katz & Kott. (2018) Rock and Roll Explorer Guide to New York, p. 152.
