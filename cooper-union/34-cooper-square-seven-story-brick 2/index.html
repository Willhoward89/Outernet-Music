34 Cooper SquareSeven Story Brick

Every line traces one seven‑story brick building through over 130 years — raised in 1894, home to Barth & Son by 1916, then the eccentric Great American Salvage Company selling everything from a disassembled Stanford White house to Fatal Attraction’s bathroom fixtures, next door to the pink‑brick offices where The Village Voice ran until 2017.
