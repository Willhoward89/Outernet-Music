37 East 4th StreetIonic Line, East Fourth

Every line traces one rowhouse through nearly 180 years — the Samuel Tredwell Skidmore House, raised around 1844–45 beside the Astor estate’s fashionable blocks, its Ionic portico and rusticated stoop surviving light industry, sculptor Hugo Robus’s residency, and a 1987 demolition scare next door, before a 2010 renovation folded it into the modern Cooper Square scheme.
