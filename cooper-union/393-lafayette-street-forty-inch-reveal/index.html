393 Lafayette StreetForty‑Inch Reveal

Every line traces one printing house through 140 years — the DeVinne Press Building, Babb, Cook & Willard’s 1885 masterwork of load‑bearing brick with walls three feet thick and forty‑inch window reveals, where Theodore Low De Vinne printed Scribner’s and The Century on his own presses, landmarked in 1966 and standing at Lafayette and East 4th today.
