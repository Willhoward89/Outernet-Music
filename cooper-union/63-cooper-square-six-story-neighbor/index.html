69 Cooper Square

"Was built in 1984." Unknown Author. Oasis Map, p. 1.

02

“'85 they call it acceptable, a six-story slow / climb of dark brick, Kudroff Rycar signed the deed, / tiers of fire balconies stacked like a ladder to breathe”

69 Cooper Square

"[E 21a.] 69 Cooper Square (apartments), on Third Ave. bet. E. 7th St and St. Mark's Place. E side. 1985. Kudroff Rycar Assocs. The tiers of fire balconies and dark brick make this an acceptable 6-story neighbor." AIA. (1988) AIA Edition 3, p. 164.

03

“Norman's Sound in the basement where the jazz vinyl rests — / sixty-seven the address, milk crates and a sale, / dollar CDs psychotically cheap when the racks get stale, / world music, used books at the bottom of the crate, / easy to overlook, down the stair, past the gate”

67 Cooper Sq

"Norman's Sound 67 Cooper Sq... Norman carries everything, but especially jazz and world music... The easy to overlook basement is crammed full of major CD, vinyl, and DVD deals. The pickin's become psychotically cheap during Norman's frequent $1 CD sales. They also have a smattering of used books, almost as if they discovered a couple of titles at the bottom of a milk crate full of CDs." Halliday. (2011) Zinester's Guide to NYC, p. 171.

04

“Café Zaiya at sixty-nine, rice-doughnut light, / that stretchy unborn texture in the food-court white, / brightly-lit and alien, chocolate-dipped and strange”

69 Cooper Sq

"69 Cooper Sq (@E 7th St)... There's something vaguely alien about doughnuts made from pounded rice. They've got this stretchy unborn bathmat texture that's counterintuitive in doughnut land... particularly the chocolate-dipped version. I'm not too down with Café Zaiya's brightly-lit food court vibe." Halliday. (2011) Zinester's Guide to NYC, p. 65.
