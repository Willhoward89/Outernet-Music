# 888 Broadway — A Palimpsest of American Ambition

The W. & J. Sloane Building of 1881–82, William Wheeler Smith's "Commercial Palace" at the
south-east corner of Broadway and East Nineteenth Street, and the store — ABC Carpet & Home — that
has occupied it since.

Nine chapters, from the Tiebout Williams farm to the building as a living institution.

**All 159 quotes are sourced; 157 of them are clickable** and open their scanned page.

**Repository path:** `brick-cakes/888-broadway/`

## Firsts for the series

- **35 works across 41 pages** — the widest range in the series, past the 31 works of 881–887.
- **159 highlights** — the most in any document here.

## How it reads

**The citation chart is on the left; the video is on the right.**

- **Left — the citation chart.** It follows you as you scroll, and jumps to the matching entry when
  you hover a highlight. Click a number to go to that quote in the text, or *scan* to open the page.
  It divides into the document's nine chapters.
- **Centre — the text.** 159 highlighted quotes.
- **Right — the video.** A 2 min 50 s introduction under the heading *A word on the building*. The
  column is sticky, so it stays beside you as you scroll.

The side columns are sticky and stack above the text below ~1360px.

Notes for the record:

- **One page is still without a scan** — Lown & Lown, *All New York*, 1997, p. 347, two quotes
  (*French and Spanish spoken* / *10% trade discount*). Both are sourced and colour-coded; drop the
  page into `assets/scans/` under that exact name and rebuild to reach 159 of 159.
- **Twenty quotes sit on the page after the one they are filed under.** The Strausbaugh scan ends
  mid-sentence at *"…target Edwin's well-known address. Edwin"*, so quotes 33–42 are on p. 281; the
  Hennessy scan ends at *"No new stores were built along Broadway"*, so quotes 69 and 71–82 are on
  p. 101. Every highlight opens the right book at the right passage's beginning, but a reader
  checking those twenty against the page image will not find them on it. Adding p. 281 and p. 101 as
  second scans would close the gap without disturbing the chart.
- **One quote does not match its scan.** Highlight 15 reads *"When his wife had contract
  pneumonia"*; Strausbaugh p. 280 prints **contracted**. The dropped *-ed* is this document's, not
  the book's.
- **Two sources disagree on the founder.** Williams & Radlauer p. 133 has *Sam* Weinrib founding the
  business in 1897 and his **granddaughter** joining in the 1980s; Leffel & Lehman p. 252 has *Max*
  Weinrib and his **great-granddaughter** running it today. Each is quoted as printed.
- **Two sources date the Lincoln assassination to August 1865.** Morgan p. 71 and Plumb p. 227 both
  print August 14; Lincoln was shot on 14 April. The highlights carry it as printed — the error is
  the sources'.
- **Lockwood p. 292 prints *comer* for *corner*, three times.** The scan itself shows *comer*, so
  text and evidence agree; it is almost certainly an rn→m artifact in the edition scanned rather
  than something Lockwood published.
- **Hennessy 82 has an unclosed parenthesis** — *"(birds, swags, cupids. rich foliage are charming"*
  — with a period where a comma belongs. As printed.
- **Eight cartographic pages came from the series scan pool** — Sackersdorff 1815 p. 3, Perris 1853
  p. 56, Dripps 1867 p. 7, Robinson 1885 p. 15, Bromley 1897 p. 19, the 1916 Atlas p. 55, the 1930
  Land Book p. 54 and the 1955 Land Book p. 44. The series spells these citations both with and
  without the year; this document uses the no-year form throughout, because that is how the existing
  scan files are named.
- **The De Leeuw p. 323 plate photographs the 888–900 block whole**, with M'Gibbon, Henry Bosch and
  Shoninger Bros. named on the awnings. It is a scan, but it earns a place among the views.
- **The five photographs were removed** when the video took the right-hand column. They are kept
  outside the repository and can be restored below the video at any time.
- **The video does not autoplay, and that is deliberate.** It carries its own spoken commentary and
  the document already has a 43-minute narration; two voices at once serves no one. It shows a
  poster frame and plays with sound when the reader presses it.
- **The video is hidden in print.** A frame of it in the PDF would be a still of a man standing
  still.
- **The PDF is deliberately not in this folder.** A 96-page print version exists and sits beside the
  folder on the Desktop; including it pushed the package to 44 MB. The folder as it stands is 28 MB,
  with no single file over 13 MB.

To replace or add a scan, drop it into `assets/scans/` named exactly as the citation reads and
rebuild.
