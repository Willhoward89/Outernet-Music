# 889 Broadway — Silver, Secrets, and Crockery

Edward Hale Kendall's Gorham Manufacturing Company Building of 1883–84 at the north-west corner of
Broadway and Nineteenth Street: a Queen Anne silver store with bachelor apartments above, later a
Soviet military-intelligence front, and now Fishs Eddy.

**All 131 quotes are sourced; 127 of them are clickable** and open their scanned page. **63 are
marked on the page itself**, so clicking the highlight shows where the passage sits on the scan.

**Repository path:** `brick-cakes/889-broadway/`

## How it reads

**The citation chart is on the left; the narration is on the right.**

- **Left — the citation chart.** It follows you as you scroll, and jumps to the matching entry when
  you hover a highlight. Click a number to go to that quote, or *scan* to open the page.
- **Centre — the text.** 131 highlighted quotes, colour-coded by source.
- **Right — the narration.** Press play and a cursor travels through the text in step with the
  voice, word by word. *Follow the reading* keeps the spoken line in view; scrolling yourself
  suspends it for a couple of seconds. Click any word outside a highlight to jump the video there.
- **The score plays independently.** Nothing in the page starts, stops or mutes either one.

The side columns are sticky and stack above the text below ~1360px.

Notes for the record:

- **Four pages are still without a scan**, one quote each: AIA *Edition 3* p. 188, White &
  Willensky *AIA Edition 1* p. 95, and Frank pp. 361 and 363. All four are sourced and
  colour-coded; drop the pages into `assets/scans/` under those exact names and rebuild to reach
  131 of 131.
- **Nine quotes sit on the page after the one they are filed under.** The Stern p. 719 scan ends
  mid-sentence at *"…easing the transition between the"*, so quotes 48, 49 and 52–58 — the gable
  dormers, the entrance pediment, and the whole Van Rensselaer / *Real Estate Record* exchange — are
  on p. 720. The highlights open the right book at the right passage, but a reader checking those
  nine against the page image will not find them on it.
- **Four more are one page early, and the page is already here.** Quotes 85, 86 and 87 (Koval's
  birth, the family's return to the USSR, the baseball remark) and the photo caption, quote 91, are
  filed to Melton p. 142 but print on p. 143, which is on file. Say the word and I will refile those
  four so they open — and mark on — the page they are actually on.
- **Five more run over.** Hennessy p. 101 ends mid-entry, so quotes 46, 51 and 69 continue on
  p. 102; the Halliday p. 195 entry breaks at *"but their own Manhattan Blue Plate Special pat-"*,
  so quotes 117 and 118 — the skyline-and-floor-plan designs and the Josh Saitz sign-off — are on
  p. 196.
- **Quotes 59 and 60 are the same sentence in two editions.** White & Willensky, *AIA Edition 1*
  (1968) p. 95 and AIA *Edition 3* (1988) p. 188 — the later one adds *"Gorham, of course,
  manufactured silverware."* They are the last two book pages outstanding, and they need separate
  scans.
- **Seven quotes are unmarked though plainly on their page.** Harris p. 157 is a photograph with pen
  annotations across it, and quotes 35, 36, 68 and 131 could not be read well enough to place. On
  Stokes p. 98 the Duane paragraph carrying quotes 5 and 6 sits under grey highlight bands and the
  orange ring; the OCR skips from the heading straight to *A. The Four Acre Parcel*, and a
  contrast-boosted re-read of that corner only turned the bands black. Court p. 222 is set sideways,
  so it was read rotated upright and the boxes turned back — but only four of quote 96's five words
  came through, one short of the floor. All seven highlights still open their page.
- **Sixty-four quotes are unmarked on their scans.** Most are map labels, headings and addresses of
  four words or fewer; the matcher refuses runs under five words, and runs covering less than a
  quarter of the quote, rather than risk drawing the box over the wrong line. Two false positives
  were caught that way: a stray *"the Real Estate Record and Builders' Guide"* in Stern's left-hand
  column, and a fragment of the Stuyvesant paragraph in Stokes p. 144.
- **Nine cartographic and directory pages came from the series scan pool** — Sackersdorff 1815 p. 3,
  Perris 1853 p. 56, Dripps 1867 p. 7, Robinson 1885 p. 15, Bromley 1897 p. 19, the 1916 Atlas
  p. 54, the 1930 Land Book p. 53, the 1955 Land Book p. 43 and Putnam's Sons p. 116. This document
  uses the no-year form of those citations throughout, because that is how the pooled files are
  named.

### What this unblocks elsewhere

- **Hennessy, *Walking Broadway*, p. 101 is the page 888 Broadway has needed all along.** Ten of its
  quotes are filed under p. 100 but print on p. 101. The scan is now in this folder and can be
  copied straight into `brick-cakes/888-broadway/assets/scans/`.
- **White & Willensky, *AIA Edition 1*, p. 95** is outstanding here; **p. 94 of the same book** is
  outstanding on 881–887 Broadway. One opening of one book closes both.

To replace or add a scan, drop it into `assets/scans/` named exactly as the citation reads and
