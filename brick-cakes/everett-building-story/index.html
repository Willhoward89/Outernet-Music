Chapter One: When the Fancy Folks Ran Away!

Once upon a time, a very long time ago, there was a bustling little neighborhood called Union Square. Back then, (lots of rich families lived in beautiful brick houses). But then something surprising happened — (shops started popping up everywhere, right in the middle of the fancy homes)! 🏪

The wealthy families were SO shocked! They huffed and they puffed and (they packed up all their belongings and zoomed away to a different neighborhood) where they hoped no shops would ever follow them. Soon, a newspaper peeked around and reported that (almost all the fancy houses had turned into cozy boardinghouses) where lots of people lived together.

But you know who LOVED this busy, bustling spot? Hotels! Big, grand, sparkly hotels! Everybody agreed (Union Square was the very best place in the whole city to build a wonderful hotel). And (the land there became worth more and more money — buckets and buckets of it)! 💰

Chapter Two: Hello, Magnificent Hotel!

And now, meet the shining star of our story — (a grand hotel called the Everett House! It was built way back in the 1850s, right on a special corner by the park). 🏨✨

Oh, it was a fancy place! (Writers came to visit. Actors came to visit. People who loved books and plays came to visit). The hotel was (so big it could hold up to 250 guests all at once) — that's a LOT of beds to make! And here's the most magical part: in 1882, the Everett House became (the very first hotel in the whole city to turn on twinkling electric lights)! Imagine the glow! 💡

The hotel got (its name from a very important man named Edward Everett, who was a famous speaker and leader). He even (gave the hotel a painting of himself that hung on the wall for years) and years! The old guidebooks gushed about the Everett House, (calling it a magnificent hotel that felt both fancy AND cozy, like a quiet private home). (They even told you exactly what time dinner was served)!

And on extra-special nights? (The street right outside the hotel was lit up with colorful, glowing lamps). How dreamy! 🌈

Chapter Three: The Big Surprise Party That Fizzled

One exciting night, a huge crowd squished into the Everett House hoping to throw a giant celebration! It was (election night, way back in 1876, and lots of people came to cheer for a man named Samuel Tilden), who they hoped would become President. 🎉

But oh no... (the party didn't go the way everyone planned). There was a big, tricky decision, and in the end, Tilden did NOT get to be President after all. (Instead, a man named Hayes won). Womp womp. All those party hats for nothing! 🎩

Chapter Four: Out With the Old, Up With the New!

Years went by, and the whole city got busier and busier. (Builders were everywhere, knocking down old buildings and digging big, deep holes) to make way for tall new ones! 🏗️

And so, (the beloved old Everett House hotel finally said its goodbyes. In its place rose a brand-new hero — the tall and mighty Everett Building!) (It was built in 1908 and it stretched up 16 whole stories into the sky)! 🌇

(The clever architect who designed it had learned all his best building tricks in a faraway city) before coming to make this one. The building was (super strong, super safe from fire, and had big beautiful windows) to let the sunshine in. (It stood proudly right beside the park), like a tall, friendly bookend watching over everything.

Chapter Five: The Prettiest Dressed-Up Building

Up close, the Everett Building is just gorgeous! It's (decorated with sparkly ornaments made from a special kind of baked clay in lots of colors). There are (leafy, fancy shapes) and (a row of little toothy decorations trimming the very top edge), almost like the building is giving a great big smile! 😁

And the best part? This building was so special that it (got a shiny badge of honor making it a protected landmark) — that means it will be cared for and kept safe for a very, very long time. Hooray! 🏅

Chapter Six: All the Friends Who Lived Inside

Over the years, SO many interesting folks filled up the rooms of this tall building! 🏢

There were (kind helpers who assisted people coming to the city from faraway places). There was (a shop absolutely bursting with magazines to read). There was (a fancy clothing store full of snazzy outfits for grown-ups). There was even (a cozy bookshop way up high that sold wonderful old books)! 📚

And there was (a spa splashed all over with a cheery, bright red color) — (the perfect place to feel fancy and relaxed before a big special day). 💅

And so, (our tall terra-cotta friend still stands by the park today), full of a hundred years of stories, watching the whole busy city hustle and bustle below.

The End! 🌟
