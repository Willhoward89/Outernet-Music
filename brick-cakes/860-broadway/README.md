# 860 Broadway

*From Sapokanican to Silkscreens: The Many Lives of a Union Square Corner* — an annotated, sourced
text on the **Parish Building** (Detlef Lienau, 1883–84; remodelled 1925 by F. H. Dewey & Company),
and on the ground it stands on: the Sir Peter Warren farm, the Manhattan Bank's square acre, the
Union Place Hotel, Sypher & Co.'s antiques, Thonet Brothers' bentwood furniture, Butler Brothers,
and Andy Warhol's fourth Factory.

Every quoted passage is highlighted and colour-coded by source. **All 59 quotes are clickable** —
each opens its scanned source page in a panel beside the text.

**Repository path:** `brick-cakes/860-broadway/`

## How it reads

Three columns on a wide screen:

- **Left — "The building today."** Five views; click any one to enlarge it full-screen.
- **Centre — the text.** 59 highlighted quotes, colour-coded by source, each one clickable.
- **Right — the citation chart.** It follows you as you scroll, and jumps to the matching entry when
  you hover a highlight. Click a number to go to that quote in the text, or *scan* to open the page.

Both side columns are sticky; below ~1360px they stack beneath the text.

This is the only one of the four documents with no audio track.

## Sources

32 works are cited across 43 pages, **every one on file**. The colour key lists them all with page
numbers; a ⌕ marks those with a scan, which here is all of them.

Notes for the record:

- **Four atlas plates are shared with the 44 Union Square East document** — Robinson *1885*, Bromley
  *1897*, G W Bromley *1916* and Bromley *1955*. 860 Broadway and 44 Union Square East sit on the
  same sheets; the block bounded by Broadway, East 17th, East 18th and Fourth Avenue is drawn on
  each.
- **LPC, *Union Square*, 1884, p. 1** — a citation record stands in for the page image. Its six
  fields supply six separate highlights in the architectural paragraph.
- **Lockwood, *Manhattan Moves Uptown*, pp. 151 and 290** place the Union Place Hotel at the
  south-east corner of **Fourteenth** Street. 860 Broadway is at Seventeenth. The error is
  Lockwood's, carried through faithfully; both pages are on file and say so.
- **Kiedrowski, *Andy Warhols New York*, pp. 97 and 102** share one supplied image.

To replace or add a scan, drop it into `assets/scans/` named exactly as the citation reads
(e.g. `Gopnik, Warhol, 2020, p. 877.jpg`) and rebuild.
