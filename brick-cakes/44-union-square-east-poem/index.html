I. The Ground

Before the brickO, before the nameO the ground held only quiet claimO— (reference to 0 structures,)V bareO, a page of Union PlaceO laid thereO.

Then dwellings rose,O (first class)V in stoneO, with (skylights)V of their very ownO; a hotel took the corner nextO— the (West Moreland,)A on the deed embossedO— and where the (Clarendon Hotel)V once stoodO the future gathered, dark and goodO.

II. The Society

A brotherhoodO, (an outgrowth of the patriotic Sons of Liberty)V, they called themselvesO (The Society of St. Tammany or Columbian Order,)V freeO; they took (a sachem of the Delaware)A for their signO, oneO (of the chiefs of the Algonquins)V down the lineO, whoO (held undisputed sway over Eastern America)A of oldO.

Then came theO (Boss)V—the infamousO (William Tweed)V— whose fallO would let aO (New Tammany)V proceedO.

III. The Building

They hired the hands ofO (Thompson, Holmes & Converse)V andO (Charles B. Meyers)V to reverseO a shabby nameO with (a dignified architectural treatment,)V (severe Colonial columns)V for embellishmentO, of (red Harvard brick)V andO (terra cotta and limestone)A, (specially molded and oversized brick laid in Flemish bond)V aloneO, with (double-height pilasters,)V aO (rusticated stone base,)A and up above,O (Hipped Roof Changing to Shell Dome)V in placeO.

A (striking resemblance)A they would castO to (Federal Hall)V and Washington's pastO— the very groundO whereO (George Washington took the oath of office)A— a mask of marble for a house of graftO.

IV. The Reputation

They wore the mask to hide the stainO of (graft, corruption, and misused power)V—the old refrainO; and yet the coffersO gave: toO (Japan's earthquake sufferers,)V theO (Cuban Freedom Fund,)V theO (Irish Bond Issue,)V its offerersO claimed grace—O(revered... feared... hated)A all at onceO, (so eulogized or so condemned)V for monthsO, nowO (a byword for municipal)A shameO, and yet they built it just the sameO.

V. The Fall

(Tammany reached its zenith)A in its hourO— thenO (Seabury)V came to test the towerO; (Walker was forced to resign)A and goO, andO (La Guardia)V led theO (coalition)A blowO that heldO (for 12 years)A its groundO; the fortunes fell;O (unable to meet)A the poundO of mortgage,O theO (sachems sold their building)A downO.

VI. The Afterlives

To (Local 91)V of theO (International Ladies Garment Workers Union)V it passedO, a hall of laborO, needle-work and mastO; theO (Roosevelt Auditorium)V rangO till (Roundabout)V came in and sangO— thenO (moved to 45th Street)A and awayO, andO (Vanessa Redgrave)V took the stage one dayO, withO (Glenn Close)V, (John Turturro)V, a benefit for the shelledO— theO (children of Sarajevo)V heldO.

AndO (the New York Film Academy,)V brightO, wouldO (Learn Film Making)V in the lightO, (Carpe Diem)V carved above the doorO where bosses schemed and schemed beforeO.

VII. The Square

Outside,O the Square was never tameO— (anarchists, socialists, Wobblies, and Communists)V made their claimO, aO (workers protest of 35,000)A massedO, andO Nellie BlyO lectured on herO (record-breaking trip around the globe)V at lastO.

And hereO (the last home of Jeannette L. Gilder)A was setO, theO (editor of the Critic)A we forgetO.

VIII. The Remnant

AO (phantom)V in theO (crawl space)V stayedO, till (the sounds stopped)V and the racketO frayedO; above the SquareO, (a liberty cap)A (eminently visible)V on the mapO, across from where theO (Charles F. Murphy Monument)V stands nearO theO (Independence Flagstaff)V, year on yearO.

TheO (sole physical remnant)V of it hereO— (shabby as it is,)V and dearO, aO (big part of our past)V it keepsO, its liberty cap while the city sleepsO.

One wag proposed, then shook his headO: aO (Museum of Political Corruption)A for the deadO— (it just wouldn't be large enough,)V he saidO.
