# 33 East 17th Street — poetic version

*The Century Building: An Ode in Brick and Stone* — a fourth treatment of William Schickel's Century
Building of 1880–81, written as verse. The scholarship is unchanged: every quoted passage is still
word-for-word from the historical sources, highlighted and colour-coded exactly as in the other
versions. What changes is the setting — the sources' own phrases are worked into metre, so a
surveyor's line about brick courses becomes part of a stanza.

Nine movements, from invocation to benediction, with four sub-sections reading the façade feature by
feature.

**All 87 quotes are clickable** — each opens its scanned source page in a panel beside the text.

**Repository path:** `brick-cakes/33-east-17th-street-4/`

This version quotes almost exactly the same material as the all-ages text, with two differences: it
restores the pair of stylistic cousins (the Gramercy apartments and the Mercantile Exchange, both
Morrone p. 116) that v3 dropped, and it omits the restoration date `1994-95`, since the verse names
no date in quotation.

It needed no new scans. All 17 pages were already on file.

## How it reads

**The citation chart is on the left**, as in v2 and v3. The photograph rail — empty for now — sits on
the right.

- **Left — the citation chart.** It follows you as you scroll, and jumps to the matching entry when
  you hover a highlight. Click a number to go to that quote in the text, or *scan* to open the page.
  It divides into the document's twelve labelled movements and sub-sections.
- **Centre — the text.** 87 highlighted quotes across nine movements.

The side column is sticky and stacks above the text below ~1360px.

Notes for the record:

- **This document was assembled without a citation chart.** Every one of the 87 quotes resolved
  against the finished v2 and v3 mappings. The chart supplied afterwards confirmed all 87 with no
  disagreements — so the scan pool and citation history for this building are now complete enough
  that a further treatment needs only its text.
- **Verse punctuation sits outside the highlights.** The text hangs possessives and compounds off
  quoted terms — `(Wyoming Bluestone)'s time`, `(Dentil Course)'s ranks`, `(Gambrel Roof)'s embrace`,
  `low-(Arched)`. In each case the highlight covers the source's words only, and the poet's addition
  stays in the surrounding line.
- **The text preserves its sources' own errors**, as printed: *Queen Anle pile*, *the braiding was
  converted*, *fames Cohen*, *die character*, *consider able original woodwork*.
- **The parenthesis parser ignores brackets in headings**, a change made for the all-ages version and
  carried here.
- **The LPC record card carries 32 of the 87 quotes**, as in every version of this building.

To replace or add a scan, drop it into `assets/scans/` named exactly as the citation reads and
rebuild.
