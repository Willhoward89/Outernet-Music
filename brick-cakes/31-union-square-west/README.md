# 31 Union Square West

*The Bank of the Metropolis* — an annotated, sourced text on Bruce Price's 1902–03 skyscraper at the
north-west corner of Sixteenth Street and Union Square West, and on the ground it stands on: an empty
lot in 1815, three first-class dwellings by 1853, the White Manufacturing Company, and then the bank
founded in 1871 whose directors included Tiffany, Steinway and Sloane.

Eleven sections, from the Blue Book of 1815 to the Blue Water Grill. The last covers 21 East 16th
Street across the way — Brownie's, where Warhol ate after the shooting, and Danny Meyer's Union
Square Cafe that replaced it.

Every quoted passage is highlighted and colour-coded by source. **65 of 69 quotes are clickable** —
each opens its scanned source page in a panel beside the text.

**Repository path:** `brick-cakes/31-union-square-west/`

## How it reads

**The citation chart is on the left in this document**, unlike the others in the series, which carry
it on the right. The photograph rail — empty for now — sits on the right.

- **Left — the citation chart.** It follows you as you scroll, and jumps to the matching entry when
  you hover a highlight. Click a number to go to that quote in the text, or *scan* to open the page.
- **Centre — the text.** 69 highlighted quotes, colour-coded by source.

The side column is sticky and stacks above the text below ~1360px.

## Sources

34 works are cited across 43 pages, **39 on file**. The colour key lists them all with page numbers;
a ⌕ marks those with a scan. This is the most heavily sourced document in the series — the tail of
restaurant and directory listings in sections X and XI draws on eight separate pages of Frank's
*Where to Find It* alone.

Not yet on file — four pages, four quotes:

- **Bromley, *1930 Land Book of the Borough of Manhattan*, p. 53** — quote 36, the 17-storey entry
- **Marquis, *Who's Who in the East Second Edition*, 1948, p. 972** — quote 42, the painter's address
- **Frank, *Where to Find It Buy It Eat It In New York*, 1980, p. 77** — quote 51, Blue Water Grill
- **Forlie, *The New York City Almanac*, 1994, p. 336** — quote 63, the Union Square Cafe entry

Notes for the record:

- **Two scans are shared with other documents in the series.** Wolfe, *New York 15 Walking Tours*,
  p. 252 comes from the Union Square Big Wigs document; LPC, *Union Square*, 1903, p. 1 comes from
  the granite document. Both were already filed under the exact citation filename.
- **Quote 69 carries one of four possible pages.** The source chart gave Frank pp. 85, 48, 55 and 57
  as four separate index entries for the same phrase; a highlight can hold only one citation, so
  p. 85 is used. The other three are not represented.
- **Several page numbers in rows 63–69 were supplied as uncertain** — Forlie p. 336 and Frank pp. 39,
  35, 42, 62, 85. They are used as given and should be checked against the books.
- **The scans have not been verified to contain their quotes.** A page can be the right page number
  from the right book and still stop before the sentence filed to it, which is what happened on three
  pages of the Union Square Big Wigs document. That check has not been run here.
- **The AIA Guide 3rd edition** is filed here as *AIA Edition 3*, matching the granite document, but
  the 33 East 17th Street and Big Wigs documents use two other spellings of the same book.
- The sources disagree on the tower's date — 1901, 1902 and 1903 all appear, and the LPC designation
  report says 1902–03. All are carried through as printed.

To replace or add a scan, drop it into `assets/scans/` named exactly as the citation reads
(e.g. `Marquis, Who's Who in the East Second Edition, 1948, p. 972.jpg`) and rebuild.
