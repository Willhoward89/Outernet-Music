The Principal Series

brick-cakes26 pages

The flagship collection, and the one still in active use. It began as the Union Square and Broadway holdings — 842, 860, 862–872, 881–887, 888 and 889 Broadway, 1–3 and 31 Union Square West, 44 Union Square East, 7 East 14th Street, 33 East 17th Street — and has since taken in work from other neighbourhoods and other boroughs. Several addresses appear more than once because a building was treated in more than one form: a song, a story, a poem. Every document built in the current working period is being published here, whatever part of the city it describes.

The Cooper Union Survey

cooper-union66 pages

The largest collection by a wide margin: a block-by-block treatment of the ground around Cooper Square and Astor Place, running out along Second and Third Avenues, East 4th and East 7th Streets, Lafayette Street, Taras Shevchenko Place and the Broadway frontage from 726 to 800. The survey is dense enough that single addresses carry several documents — 7 East 7th Street has four, including a rondo — and it reaches beyond its own boundaries to Sag Harbor and Main Street, which suggests it also served as a general holding area during a period of rapid work.

The Sag Harbor Village Survey

sag-harbor35 pages

A village-wide survey of the Long Island whaling port: Main Street from number 1 to number 221, the Bay Street waterfront, Division, Madison, Rector, Church, Hampton and Washington Streets. The subject matter runs from the whale-oil trade and the windmills on the shore to the Watchcase factory and the fires that repeatedly took the village. Two further Sag Harbor documents — the Pharmacy and the John Jermain Memorial Library — were built later and published to the principal series instead.

Discrepancies in the Record

Five things worth settling before this catalogue is relied on as authoritative.

The index is out of date.
outernet-music-urls.txt was last written on 14 October and records 140 pages. Every document built since is missing from it, and three of its entries point at paths that have been superseded. It should be regenerated from the repository rather than trusted.
Two paths disagree for the same document.
The index files Clinton Hall Fire under cooper-union, Ground Beneath the Bowery under cooper-union, The Berkeley Rise under fifth-avenue and Thimble Theatre Dreams under fifth-avenue. All four have since been built for the principal series instead. Either the older paths are stale, or both exist and the older one serves an earlier version.
One address is filed in two collections.
41 Cooper Square — “Iron and Pixel” — appears under both cooper-union and sag-harbor. The Cooper Square filing is the plausible one.
One folder name contains spaces.
34 Division Street — “Brick and Fire” — is the only path in the repository with spaces rather than hyphens, so its URL carries %20 escapes. It resolves, but it is the one entry that will break if a link is ever copied by hand.
One page was reported missing.
brick-cakes/american-hotel returned 404 when checked during this working period, although the index lists it. A prepared folder for it exists on disk.
